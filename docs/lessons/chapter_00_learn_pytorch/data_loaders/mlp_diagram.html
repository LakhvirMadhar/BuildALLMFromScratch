Neurons, layers, and Linear

Each circle is a neuron. Each column of circles is a layer. Each line is one weight. This is a shrunken version of your network (2 → 3 → 2). Yours is 2 → 30 → 20 → 2, built the same way. Hover or tap a neuron to see what it computes.

Hover a green or orange circle. Its incoming lines light up, and you'll see its formula and which row of the weight matrix it owns.

The blue circles on the left are just your two feature values. They don't compute anything.
