Sigmoid Explorer

Drag the sliders to change the inputs of z = x1 * w1 + b, then watch where z lands on the S-curve and what loss it produces. Starts at the values in 01_logistic_regression_forward_pass.py.

The formula, with your numbers

z = x1 * w1 + b
e^(-z)
1 + e^(-z)
a = 1 / (1 + e^(-z))
slope = a * (1 - a)
